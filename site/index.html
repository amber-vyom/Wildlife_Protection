How it works

From your report to the animal's release, every step is tracked.

1

Report

Tell us where the animal is and what you see. Add a photo or map location if you can.

2

Rescue

A coordinator sends a field agent, who captures the animal and brings it to the centre.

3

Treat

Vets diagnose and treat it. Caretakers feed, monitor and log its recovery every day.

4

Release

With Forest Department permission, healthy animals go back to the wild and can be tracked.

Before you report: keep a safe distance, don't feed or handle the animal, and keep pets and people away. If someone is in danger, call your local emergency number first.

Questions

Do I need an account to report an animal?

No. Reporting is open to everyone and no personal details are stored. You get a tracking code to check progress.

How do I follow my report?

Open the Track page and enter the 8-character code you received after submitting.

Who can create an account?

Anyone can sign up as a donor or volunteer. Staff accounts are created by the centre's coordinator.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are stored as salted hashes, accounts lock after repeated failed sign-ins, and sessions time out when idle.

What happens to the animal afterwards?

Once the vet clears it and the Forest Department grants a permit, it is released or moved to a sanctuary. Released animals can be tracked.
